**Summary**

The square is saturated in every direction the brief listed except one. Relational (PrivLava, PrivPetal), continual release and versioning, fairness, rare events, profiler auditing, discretization, implementation and floating-point auditing, hyperparameter-selection leakage, and small-n inferential validity all have named owners, and three of them belong to the very cluster the team must avoid — Ganev/Annamalai/Mahiou/De Cristofaro have now audited the privacy of three pre-synthesis preprocessing steps in eighteen months: domain extraction (ICLR-W 2025), discretization (CCS 2025), and SMOTE oversampling (ICLR 2026). The fourth universal pre-synthesis step, missing-data handling, has never been audited. The only paper that owns it, Mohapatra et al. PVLDB 17(8) 2024, is a utility paper: I pulled the full PDF and grepped it, and it contains no membership inference experiment and no privacy audit at all. Its privacy contribution runs the OTHER way — it models missingness as sampling to get an amplification bound (cost 0.88x down to 0.44x on Adult) — and Roburin/Pinot/Scornet (Feb 2026) push the same amplification line. So there is a live, unmeasured tension: amplification theory says missing data protects you; the Ganev pattern says the way you handle it breaks end-to-end DP. Nobody has run the experiment. The clincher is in the team's own repo: synthproof/data/dataset.py:102 and synthproof/api/main.py:510 both call df.dropna(axis=0, how="any"), and synthproof/data/datasets.py:230 documents dropping Adult's "?" rows "matching the convention" — they are performing un-charged, data-dependent complete-case deletion on a dataset with native MNAR-flavoured missingness, which is exactly the class of bug they have twice built their credibility on finding. RECOMMENDATION 3A (lead): audit the missing-data handling step — four arms (complete-case drop, "?"-as-category, non-private imputation, DP imputation) against their existing DOMIAS/distance-MIA/canary/GDP auditors, on Adult's native missingness plus injected MCAR/MAR/MNAR matched to Mohapatra's setup. Two weeks, no new models, no GPU. The one thing that can kill it is the audit ceiling they already discovered: they must demonstrate their auditor resolves a known leak at the chosen n BEFORE running the grid, or a null result will be uninterpretable. RECOMMENDATION 3B (companion, three days, near-zero risk): show that the standard DP-SDG evaluation protocol is structurally blind to complete-case selection bias, because every paper filters incomplete rows out of the ground truth it evaluates against, and measure the magnitude on Adult and ACSIncome. Framed as a statistics claim it dies to Little and Rubin 1987; framed as a defect in the field's measurement instrument it is the same species of argument as their audit-ceiling finding, and it is a measurement rather than a corollary, which is the distinction that killed the last one. A runner-up I deliberately did not promote: folktables' own datasheet states it treats ACS records at person level only, so the ACSIncome DP-synthesis literature does record-level DP on a household-clustered sample — striking, but an examiner will call it group privacy, and corollary-shaped claims are precisely how two of their eleven claims died.
