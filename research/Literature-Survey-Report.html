P1 Stadler et al. 2022
✔
What they did
First quantitative proof that synthetic data leaks membership for outlier records; open-source attack framework.
✗
The gap
No signed or checkable release artifact; the DP fix needs a public dataset most curators lack.
◉
SynthProof fills it
A data-blind linter + signed sheet that flags the very leak (domain/seed) they found.
P2 Annamalai et al. 2024
✔
What they did
Tight empirical auditing of 6 DP synthesizers; black-box MIAs read ε≈0 even at true ε=4.
✗
The gap
Audits the mechanism — needs code, model internals & worst-case data; not the shipped document.
◉
SynthProof fills it
Grounds our audit ceiling; explains why our audited ε=0.000 is loose, not ‘safe’.
P3 Cebere et al. 2026
✔
What they did
‘Re:cord-play’ grey-box audit: 13 privacy violations across 12 DP libraries.
✗
The gap
Audits library code, not the released artifact/document.
◉
SynthProof fills it
Same ‘neighbours + identical randomness’ trick as our seed-replay; motivates our 2nd accountant.
P4 Ganev et al. 2025
✔

SynthProof fills it

Where each paper sits

The release boundary — and who guards what

Differential privacy protects only the machine in the middle. A real deployment ships a file, and it can leak before the machine (preprocessing) and after it (the label). Each paper studies one stretch of this pipe; SynthProof adds the signed, checkable layer at the end.

Read it like this: P4/P5/P6/P7 audit the preprocessing step · P8/P2/P3 work on the mechanism · P1 & P9 expose what leaks in the shipped artifact · P10 & P11 show the field agreed what to disclose but never made it checkable. That last box is SynthProof.

Shared ground

Which paper used which dataset

UCI Adult is the shared benchmark (5 papers) — where our same-dataset comparison lives. P7 also uses Bank. ACSIncome is ours alone.

The one-line verdict. The field converged on what a DP release should disclose (P10) but never on making it checkable — no signing (P10), no limit-reporting (“privacy theater”), invariants in prose only (P9), and analysts who never verify (P11). That gap — signed + machine-checkable + limit-reporting — is SynthProof.
