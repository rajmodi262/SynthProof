SynthProof — Making a DP Synthetic-Data Release Checkable

The field agrees on what to disclose about a differentially private release, and knows the ways it leaks. What no one ships is a release a stranger can verify from the artifact alone. SynthProof builds that: a signed, machine-checkable privacy label with a 14-check release-boundary auditor — implementing the label Dibia et al. propose and filling the two gaps they leave (a signature, and honest reporting of the audit's limits).

Our contribution — three pillars

1Release-boundary auditor

RB1–RB14. Reads only the published artifact — never the data or the producer's code — and flags every channel that leaks outside ε: the run seed, exact row count, unkeyed fingerprint, unlabelled evaluation, uncharged domain/discretisation, undisclosed amplification, and multi-table (foreign-key degree, join cardinality, cross-table linkage).

2Signed, checkable label

The Privacy Data Sheet as a Croissant 1.1 extension: Dibia's 9 categories + an Ed25519 signature + an operating-range field (the two gaps Dibia leaves). Conformance = passing the auditor. Validated against the official MLCommons validator with 0 warnings.

3Gated end-to-end pipeline

One reproducible command: synthesise → sign → emit Croissant → assert RB1–RB14 clean, conformant, signature verifies, MLCommons-validates → one report with hashes. The runnable artifact an examiner can execute.

Honest novelty (checked, research/25): the leak phenomena are known and empirical MIA/GDP auditing is crowded — so we claim neither. What is not built elsewhere is the signed, artifact-only, machine-checkable checker: Dibia proposes the label and builds no checker, no signature, no limit-reporting. That is our engineering contribution.

What we deliberately do NOT claim

  • Not "15/15 parameters pass" — that figure does not replicate; we report the RB1–RB14 checks and the audit ceiling instead.
  • Not "we recover the original data" — we detect what a release leaks beyond its ε (e.g. a published seed replays membership 15/15). We never reconstruct private records.
  • Audited ε = 0 is instrument-limited, not proof of no leakage — the honest headline, and exactly why the operating-range field exists.
  • Multi-table auditing ships; multi-table synthesis does not — no validated relational generator yet (declared future work / Paper 2).
  • The generators (AIM/MST) are integrations of published methods, not novel mechanisms.
