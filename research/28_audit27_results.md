**Reading it.** The rebuilt AIM (one zCDP budget, exponential-mechanism selection, target workload)
is the source of the gain: it now clears 93% on every dataset except Nursery (80-82%), where it
went from 67-73% before. MST improves or holds on most tables but is WORSE on Bank
(87% → 81% over 3 seeds at ε=8) and slightly lower on Nursery and Breast Cancer; the best result on
each of those is AIM, so no headline number falls, but the MST regression is real and unexplained.
Texas above 100% means the synthetic-trained model matched the real-trained one within seed noise;
it is not a claim that synthetic data beats real data.

Every proved ε equals its target (1.000 / 7.9996), where the pre-fix grid spent 0.78 / 6.5.
