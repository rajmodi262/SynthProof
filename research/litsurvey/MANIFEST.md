## Provenance & Sizing Caveats

1. **Texas Hospital Discharge (P1)**: Stadler et al. sampled 50,000 records from pre-processed 2013 inpatient data. We downloaded the 100,000-row research mirror from the official EPFL GroundHog repository (`spring-epfl/synthetic_data_release`). It is a *superset* of the paper's sample. **Provenance note:** This is an open academic research mirror; it was not obtained under an individual data-use agreement with the Texas Department of State Health Services.
2. **BR2000 (P7)**: Mohapatra et al. used 38,000 records from the Brazilian 2000 census. We downloaded the 38,000-row research mirror from the official Kamino author repository (`cgebest/kamino`). **Provenance note:** This file is a research-mirror benchmark, not an authorized export from direct IPUMS International registration.
3. **National ACS Excerpts (P7)**: Mohapatra et al. evaluated on a 15,012-row subset. We downloaded the full 27,111-row 2018 excerpt (and 27,253-row 2019 excerpt) from the official NIST SDNist repository. Our local file is the official NIST parent excerpt, not the author's filtered 15,012-row table.
4. **Salary Benchmark (P8)**: `salary.csv` (135,727 records) from the DPBench / AIM benchmark distribution contains real public-record names and salaries from public disclosure data.
5. **San Francisco Fire Calls (P2, P8)**: The primary experimental benchmark is `fire.csv` (305,119 rows, 15 columns), matching P8 Table 3. The accompanying `sf_fire_calls_sample.csv` (1,000 rows) is a small DataSF Socrata API connectivity check.
6. **Census 2010/2020 CEF/HDF Microdata (P9)**: Confidential decennial census microdata (308M/331M records) restricted under Title 13 U.S.C. Access is prohibited by federal law without US Census Bureau Special Sworn Status.
7. **Interview / User Studies (P10, P11)**: P10 (12 DP experts) and P11 (15 practitioners) are qualitative studies; neither paper uses quantitative machine learning benchmark datasets.
